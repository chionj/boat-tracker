Charts show whatever the filters, search box, and Listed time range select (time range is based on when a boat was first seen by the tracker). Click a boat row or dot to highlight it; click again to clear. Green dots = new this scan; gray = gone/sold (shown in the All view as market history). Check the J / P / R boxes to mark favorites — colored rings appear around that boat on the charts, and the J / P / R buttons toggle each person’s rings on or off. Favorites save in this browser; click “Save favorites” and drop the downloaded file into boat_tracker/ to publish picks to the website.
